# Template 01: Lead Intake and Instant Follow-Up

## Problem

Small businesses often receive inquiries through forms but respond slowly, copy information manually, or lose track of potential clients.

## Solution

This n8n workflow will:

1. Receive a business inquiry
2. Validate the submitted information
3. Clean and organize the data
4. Add or update the lead in Google Sheets
5. Send a confirmation email to the lead
6. Notify the business owner

## Initial Tools

- n8n
- Google Forms
- Google Sheets
- Gmail
